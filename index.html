About

I am a PhD candidate at the School of Information Science and Technology, University of Science and Technology of China. My research focuses on brain-computer interfaces, EEG signal denoising, robust neural decoding, and medical artificial intelligence.

I am particularly interested in building EEG processing methods that are not only cleaner in waveform metrics, but also more useful for downstream decision-making tasks.

Research Interests

EEG denoising Brain-computer interfaces Robust neural decoding Self-supervised EEG pre-training Task-oriented signal cleaning Medical AI
